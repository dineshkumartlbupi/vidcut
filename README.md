# NovaCut: AI Video Editor & Reel Maker

**Application ID:** `com.novacut.videoeditor`  
**Target Android Version:** Android 15 (API 35/36)  
**Min Android Version:** Android 7.0 (API 24)  
**Target Platforms:** Android & iOS

---

## 🌟 Overview
NovaCut is a full end-to-end mobile video editor and reel maker built in Flutter. Designed to rival CapCut, VN, and InShot, it provides creators with multi-track timeline editing, cinematic color grading, sound mixing, animated titles, and hardware-accelerated 4K export.

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture

### 1. Multi-Track Timeline & Smart Splicing
- Gesture-driven, zoomable timeline with yellow trim handles.
- Millisecond-precision draggable playhead with snap-to-cut functionality.
- Split clip at playhead (`Icons.call_split`).
- Multi-clip timeline with visual filmstrips, clip durations, and transitions.

### 2. Social Canvas & Aspect Ratios
- **9:16** — TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.
- **16:9** — YouTube, Cinema widescreen.
- **1:1** — Instagram square feed.
- **4:5** — Portrait feed.
- **21:9** — CinemaScope.

### 3. Color Grading & Hollywood LUTs
- **Vibrant Pop**: Rich saturation and color depth.
- **Teal & Orange**: Hollywood blockbuster film grade.
- **Vintage 90s**: Warm retro film grain aesthetic.
- **Film Noir**: High-contrast monochrome.
- **Cyberpunk Neon**: Electric ultraviolet and cyan highlights.
- **Golden Hour**: Warm radiant sunset glow.
- **Cold Glacier**: Nordic crisp blue grade.

### 4. Audio Studio & Sound Effects (SFX)
- Royalty-free background music library (Lo-Fi Chill, Cyber Beats, Acoustic, Trap).
- Essential SFX transitions (Whoosh, Camera Shutter, Pop, Ding, Glitch).
- Device audio file import (MP3/WAV).
- Multi-channel audio mixing (0% to 200% volume slider + 1-tap Mute).

### 5. Animated Subtitles & Dynamic Text
- Add custom text layers positioned anywhere in the video frame.
- Font sizes, bold, italic, color swatches, and background pill box badges.

### 6. Stickers & Badges
- Creator stickers ("FIRE 🔥", "TRENDING 🚀", "SUBSCRIBE 🔴", "EPIC ⚡", "100 💯").

### 7. Speed & Motion Curves
- 0.25x Super Slow-Motion to 4.0x Hyperlapse.

### 8. Ultra HD 4K 60FPS Export Engine
- 480p, 720p HD, 1080p Full HD, 4K Cinema.
- 30 FPS / 60 FPS frame rate toggles.
- Live rendering progress wave.
- Save to Gallery & Direct Social Sharing.
