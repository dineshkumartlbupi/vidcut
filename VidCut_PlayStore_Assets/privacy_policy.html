Privacy Policy for VidCut

Application ID: com.vidcut.videoeditor Effective Date: November 25, 2026 Last Updated: November 25, 2026

🔒 Our Core Privacy Guarantee

VidCut operates as a local, on-device video editor. All video clips, photos, music tracks, voiceovers, and exported files are processed directly on your device's hardware. VidCut does NOT upload, transmit, store, or sell your private media content to cloud servers.

1. Introduction

Welcome to VidCut: Video Editor & Maker ("VidCut", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparent, clear information on how our app handles device permissions, user media, and technical telemetry.

This Privacy Policy applies to the VidCut mobile application available on the Google Play Store. By installing and using VidCut, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. User Media & Data Processing

VidCut provides full-featured video editing, velocity curve ramping, transition blending, color filter grading, audio extraction, and 4K 60FPS video encoding directly on your Android device:

  • On-Device Media Processing: When you import videos, photos, or music into the editor, the app accesses them locally. No media file ever leaves your device storage.
  • Project Drafts & Timelines: Your video project edits, timeline cuts, text captions, and filter selections are stored exclusively inside the app's secure private sandbox directory (/data/user/0/com.vidcut.videoeditor/).
  • No Account Required: VidCut does not require you to create an account, register an email, or log in with social media to access editing tools.

3. Android System Permissions & Purpose

To enable editing and saving video projects, VidCut requests specific Android operating system permissions. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time via Android Settings > Apps > VidCut > Permissions.

Permission Platform Scope Purpose
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO Android 13+ (API 33+) Imports user-selected video clips from your gallery into the editor timeline.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Android 13+ (API 33+) Imports photos for photo-to-video slideshow reels, thumbnails, and PIP overlays.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Android 13+ (API 33+) Imports custom MP3/WAV background music and audio tracks from your phone storage.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 12 and below Enables media import for older Android OS releases with legacy storage models.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Android 9 and below Saves rendered 1080p and 4K videos directly to your device's Gallery / Movies album.
CAMERA (Optional) All versions Used only if you explicitly choose to record live camera video directly into a project.
RECORD_AUDIO (Optional) All versions Used only if you explicitly choose to record a live microphone voiceover onto the timeline.

4. Third-Party Services & Technical Diagnostics

VidCut integrates minimal, trusted industry SDKs compliant with Google Play Developer policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Core: Used for app integrity verification, in-app updates, and split APK asset delivery. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • ExoPlayer & Android MediaCodec: Native hardware-accelerated video decoding and encoding libraries that run 100% locally on your device's GPU/CPU.
  • Anonymous Crash Telemetry: In the event of an unhandled crash or hardware encoder failure, anonymous technical diagnostics (Android OS version, device model, crash stack trace) may be collected to patch bugs. These reports contain no personal information, face data, or video content.

5. Data Selling & Advertising

VidCut does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data, media, or usage patterns with third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

7. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Under international privacy regulations (including the EU GDPR, UK GDPR, and California CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, delete, and control your personal data:

  • Right to Deletion: You can purge all project drafts, cached preview clips, and application settings by simply clearing app storage or uninstalling VidCut.
  • Zero Cloud Footprint: Because we do not store your media on servers, there is no remote data footprint associated with your identity.
  • No Sale of Personal Info: We do not sell personal data, satisfying California CCPA/CPRA requirements.
