# Privacy Policy for NovaCut: AI Video Editor & Reel Maker

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 24, 2026

NovaCut Studios ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **NovaCut: AI Video Editor & Reel Maker** mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your personal media and data.

This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles your information, media files, and permissions in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

---

## 1. On-Device Processing & Zero Media Data Collection
NovaCut is built with a **local-first, on-device architecture**:
- **Videos, Photos, and Audio Files:** All video editing, trimming, filtering, text overlays, and rendering occur locally on your device. We **do not upload, store, or transmit your personal videos or images to any external servers or cloud storage**.
- **No Account Required:** You can use all video editing tools without creating an account or providing your name, email, phone number, or passwords.

---

## 2. Permissions Used and Why They Are Needed

NovaCut requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to perform video editing tasks:

### A. Media & Storage Permissions (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`)
- **Purpose:** To allow you to browse and select your own videos, photos, and music tracks from your phone's gallery to edit inside the timeline, and to save your exported video back to your Gallery (DCIM folder).
- **Compliance:** We strictly adhere to Android 14+ granular media permissions and Scoped Storage requirements. We only access the specific media files you choose to import.

### B. Camera Permission (`CAMERA`)
- **Purpose:** Allows you to capture fresh video clips directly within NovaCut to add immediately into your editing project.
- **Usage:** Used solely during active in-app camera recording. No background camera access occurs.

### C. Microphone Permission (`RECORD_AUDIO`)
- **Purpose:** Allows you to record voiceover audio commentary over your video clips or record ambient sound when using the built-in camera.
- **Usage:** Only accessed when you explicitly tap to record voiceover or video.

### D. Internet Permission (`INTERNET`)
- **Purpose:** Allows the app to download royalty-free background music, sound effects, and sample stock video clips from verified CDN sources.

## 3. Third-Party Services
NovaCut does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not use intrusive data tracking SDKs. Any social sharing (e.g. sharing to Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, or YouTube) is initiated solely by your explicit tap via the standard Android system share sheet.

## 5. Security of Your Data
