Privacy Policy for NovaCut: AI Video Editor

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. On-Device Processing & Zero Media Data Collection

NovaCut Studios ("we", "our") is dedicated to creator privacy. NovaCut is designed with a local-first, on-device architecture. All video trimming, splitting, color grading, text compositing, and audio mixing occur strictly on your device hardware. We do not upload or store your personal videos or photos on any remote servers.

2. Permissions Used & Android 14 Compliance

  • Media & Storage (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used exclusively to import media into your editing projects and save rendered MP4 videos to your Gallery.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used solely when you record new video clips inside the app.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely when recording live voiceover commentaries over video tracks.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Used to download verified royalty-free music and stock template assets.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal content. Exported videos are only shared when you explicitly tap the system share button to post to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or WhatsApp.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding our privacy practices, reach out to privacy@novacut.app.
